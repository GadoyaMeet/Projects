## Section 3: Covenant Breaches & Legal Remedies
1. Financial Covenant Violation: Section 7.2 of the Credit Agreement mandates a minimum Fixed Charge Coverage Ratio of 1.25x. The Borrower reported 0.85x, triggering an Event of Default on February 28, 2025.
2. Cross-Default Clause: Failure to cure the $4.2M working capital revolving deficit by April 15, 2025 will trigger cross-default under the junior subordinated notes.
3. Collateral Deficit: Realizable liquidation value of warehouse collateral is appraised at $29.2M, creating an uncollateralized exposure gap of $19.3M.
