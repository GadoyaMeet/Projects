# Apex Cloud Solutions Inc. — Annual Report & Credit Review Excerpt (FY2024)

## Section 1: Executive Overview & Operating Performance
Apex Cloud Solutions Inc. ("Apex" or "the Company") is an enterprise cloud infrastructure and SaaS orchestration provider. During the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, the Company achieved annualized recurring revenue (ARR) of $95.0 million, representing a 39.7% year-over-year increase. This expansion was driven by widespread enterprise adoption of the Apex DataMesh orchestration suite.

## Section 3: Liquidity, Solvency & Debt Covenants
The Company is subject to senior credit covenants under its Credit Agreement with First Commercial Syndicate:
1. Minimum Interest Coverage Ratio: The Company must maintain an EBITDA-to-Interest expense ratio of not less than 3.00:1.00. For FY2024, the actual ratio was 8.06:1.00 (in compliance).
2. Maximum Leverage Ratio (Debt to Equity): Senior Debt to Equity must not exceed 2.25:1.00. For FY2024, the actual ratio was 0.51:1.00 (in compliance).
3. Minimum Unencumbered Liquidity: Minimum unrestricted cash of $15.0 million must be held at all times. Actual unrestricted cash was $38.6 million.

## Section 5: Risk Factors & Legal Contingencies
The Company operates in a highly competitive cloud orchestration market. Key risk factors include:
- Concentration of customer accounts: The top 5 customers represent 24% of annual recurring revenue.
- Foreign exchange volatility: 18% of billings are in EUR and GBP without active synthetic hedging.
- Litigation: No material regulatory enforcement actions or litigation pending as of December 31, 2024.
